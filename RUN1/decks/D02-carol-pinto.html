    The Concept 02 / 14
    Come see what I see.

    A person with a distinctive way of seeing the world.

    Carol Pinto is a storyteller, observer and creator. Years inside houses like Chanel, Titan and heritage luxury brands trained her eye — but this account is not her portfolio. It is her life, looked at closely.

    The audience proposition

    i.Come see what I see.

    ii.Come see what I make from it.

    The differentiator

    Not what Carol does. What Carol notices.

    Storytelling & Voice 07 / 14
    Core rule

    Reveal, don’t announce.

    The account should make people feel like they discovered Carol through what she notices, makes and chooses to show.

    Don’t sayDo this
    • “I am passionate about writing.”→Show Carol writing.
    • “I love books.”→Show the book and tell us why it stayed with her.
    • “I believe in storytelling.”→Tell a story.
    • “I’m building something.”→Let us watch it being built.
    Observed not manufacturedPersonal not promotionalCinematic not trendy
